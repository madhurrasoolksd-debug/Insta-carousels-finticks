Nvidia probes CEO Jensen Huang: what it means for Sensex, Nifty and AI stocks today 🔍

Nvidia's board said on Sunday that independent directors, working with outside counsel, will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a suspected Chinese spy. Huang admitted an affair and apologised, and he remains CEO during the review. The board says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia's secrets were compromised.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the bellwether for global AI and semiconductor stocks. Last week's AI sell-off sent the Nasdaq down 3.4% and the S&P 500 down 2.7%, while foreign investors (FIIs) bought ₹45,960 crore of Indian shares and the Nifty 50 jumped 8.6%. Early Monday, Japan's Nikkei rebounded over 2% and Brent crude eased to about $97.5 a barrel.

This week, watch US CPI inflation data on Tuesday, Fed rate hike odds (about 77% for December after a strong US jobs report) and Nvidia's Q3 results on November 17. Indian markets are open today and shut on Tuesday for Diwali Balipratipada.
