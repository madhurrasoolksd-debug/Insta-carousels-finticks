Sensex ends 773 points higher as the post-Diwali rally pares its early gains 📈

Indian markets closed higher on Nov 11, 2026: the Sensex gained 772.75 points (0.95%) to 81,879.40 and the Nifty 50 rose 210.40 points (0.84%) to 25,255.05. That is well below the ~1.5% jump seen in early trade, so the rally cooled through the session.

Why it matters: Brent crude near $92 a barrel, close to a two-month low, eases pressure on oil-importing India, while a hot US CPI print has firmed Fed December rate-hike bets and Wall Street fell overnight. Autos and banks led the gains, per HDFC Sky. FPI flows and crude are the next things to watch for the Sensex and Nifty.
