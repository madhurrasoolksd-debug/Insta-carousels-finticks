FPI buying in Indian stocks: foreign money is rushing back into Dalal Street 📈

Foreign portfolio investors bought ₹38,971 crore of Indian equities in the week to Nov 6, per NSDL data. On Nov 12, 2026 the Sensex closed at 81,911.01 (+0.04%) and the Nifty 50 at 25,299.35 (+0.18%), with realty the top sector gainer and midcaps and smallcaps outperforming.

Why it matters: lower crude oil prices, a global selloff in AI-linked stocks and improving bank and auto earnings have pushed foreign investors toward Indian large-caps. Steady FPI inflows are a key driver for the Sensex, Nifty and the rupee.
