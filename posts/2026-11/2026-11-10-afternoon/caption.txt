MakeMyTrip India gets SEBI nod for IPO: all offer for sale, no fresh issue 📈

SEBI issued its final observations on Nov 6 for the IPO of MakeMyTrip (India), the Gurugram-based arm of Nasdaq-listed MakeMyTrip, and the approval became public on Nov 9. The company filed draft papers confidentially in July. The issue will be entirely an offer for sale (OFS) by parent MakeMyTrip and its unit ibibo Group Holdings (Singapore).

Why it matters: the Indian company will not receive any proceeds. The parent and ibibo Group expect to use the net proceeds for long-term growth, acquisitions and buybacks of securities, while MakeMyTrip India stays a subsidiary and its results continue to be consolidated. Issue size, price band and dates will be announced with the red herring prospectus.
