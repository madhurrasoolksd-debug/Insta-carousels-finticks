Hot US inflation rattles Wall Street as Fed rate hike bets firm up 📉

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% economists expected, while core CPI rose 0.3% versus 0.2% forecast. On Nov 10 the Nasdaq fell 0.74%, the S&P 500 0.38% and the Dow 0.30%, giving back part of the previous day's rally.

Why it matters: this was the last US CPI report before the Federal Reserve's December meeting. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said a 50 basis point hike should be considered if core inflation came in at 0.3% or higher. Higher US interest rates for longer can pull foreign money out of emerging markets like India, so FPI flows and the Nifty and Sensex reaction on the reopen after Balipratipada are worth watching.
