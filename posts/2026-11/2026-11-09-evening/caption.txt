Sensex and Nifty close at an 8-month high as Citi and HSBC turn bullish on Indian stocks 📈

The Nifty 50 rose 0.23% to 25,044.65 and the Sensex gained 0.13% to 81,106.65 on Monday, their highest closes since February 27. PSU bank stocks led with the Nifty PSU Bank index up 3.7% (SBI +2.6%), pharma rose about 1.7%, while Nifty IT fell 1.2% as HCL Tech and Tech Mahindra slipped.

Why it matters: Citi raised its end-2027 Nifty target to 28,000, about 12% upside, citing earnings, lower oil prices and money rotating out of crowded AI trades. HSBC upgraded India to overweight from neutral and cut China to neutral. Foreign investors (FPIs) have bought more than $4.1 billion of Indian shares in November, the most in a month since September 2024, while Brent crude eased to around $97.

Markets are shut on Tuesday for Diwali-Balipratipada and reopen Wednesday. Brokerage targets are forecasts, so watch FPI flows, crude oil and the remaining Q2 results.
