Crude oil price crashes nearly 6% on Iran peace hopes as Wall Street rallies over 2% 🛢️📈

Brent crude settled down 5.85% at $92.57 a barrel on Monday and WTI fell 4.38% to $86.85, after President Trump said he was highly unlikely to order fresh attacks on Iran while peace talks progress. Brent slid more than $7 after settlement to its lowest since late August, following a 16% fall last week. The Dow Jones jumped 1,104 points (+2.15%), the S&P 500 rose 2.35% and the Nasdaq 2.74% as chip stocks led, even as Nvidia fell over 4% on a board probe.

Why it matters: India imports about 89% of its crude, so cheaper oil eases the import bill, inflation and pressure on the rupee, which closed at 93.93 per dollar on Monday. Asia followed Wall Street today: South Korea's Kospi opened up about 5.5% and SoftBank jumped 11% in Tokyo.

NSE and BSE are shut today for Diwali Balipratipada and reopen Wednesday after Sensex and Nifty closed at an 8-month high. Next up: US CPI inflation data tonight (7 PM IST), Iran talks and shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.
