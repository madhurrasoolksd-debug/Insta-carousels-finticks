GIFT Nifty points to a gap-up for Sensex and Nifty as markets reopen after Balipratipada 📈

Indian markets were shut on Nov 10, but GIFT Nifty traded about 1% higher, per Business Standard, hinting at a firmer start on Nov 11. The Nifty 50 last closed at 25,045 and the Sensex at 81,107, their highest levels since Feb 27. Brent crude fell 5.85% to $92.57 on Nov 9 as Wall Street rallied over 2%.

Why it matters: India imports about 89% of its crude, so cheaper oil on Iran peace hopes eases pressure on inflation, the rupee and the oil marketing companies. Foreign portfolio investors have bought more than $4.1 billion of Indian shares in November. US inflation data due at 7 PM IST is the next test for global sentiment.

GIFT Nifty is an indicator, not a guarantee of how the open will go.
