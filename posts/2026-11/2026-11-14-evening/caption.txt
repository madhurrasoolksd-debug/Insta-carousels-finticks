Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns, yet Wall Street ends the week higher 📈

Nvidia said on Nov 12, 2026 that CEO Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO. Chip stocks slid on Friday (AMD -8.8%, Broadcom -4.8%) and the Nasdaq fell 0.9% to 25,136, but the S&P 500 still gained 2.5% for the week, its first weekly gain since October.

Why it matters: AI stocks drive much of Wall Street's swings, Brent crude is near $96 a barrel and the US 10-year yield rose to 5.15%, with economists expecting a Fed rate hike in December. These global cues are what Indian investors and the Nifty 50 will watch when markets reopen on Monday.
