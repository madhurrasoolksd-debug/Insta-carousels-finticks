Sensex and Nifty jump on reopening after the Diwali break while Wall Street slips 📈

Indian markets reopened on Nov 11 after the Balipratipada holiday, and the Sensex rose about 1.5% to roughly 82,350 in early trade while the Nifty 50 gained about 1.3% to around 25,360. The previous close on Nov 9 was 81,107 (Sensex) and 25,045 (Nifty).

Why it matters: India rallied even though the S&P 500 fell 0.38% and the Nasdaq 0.74% on Nov 10. Brent crude near $92 a barrel, still below $100, eases pressure on an oil-importing economy, and bank stocks led the gains with the Bank Nifty up over 1%. FPI flows after the hot US inflation print and the crude price are the next things to watch for the Nifty 50 and Sensex.
