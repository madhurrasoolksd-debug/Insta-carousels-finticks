Muhurat trading 2026: Sensex and Nifty open Samvat 2083 flat 🪔

In today's one-hour Diwali Muhurat trading session, the Sensex slipped 28.62 points to 80,999.40 and the Nifty 50 fell 43.95 points to 24,986.40, back below 25,000. The Sensex had been up 414 points at its peak before the early rally faded. Sun Pharma and Tata Steel led the gains, while M&M, Infosys and IndiGo dragged.

Why it matters: Samvat 2082 was a losing year for the big indices. The Sensex fell about 4% and the Nifty about 3% from Diwali to Diwali, while the Nifty Midcap 100 gained 2.1% and the Smallcap 100 gained 4.2%. Record selling by foreign investors (FPIs) was offset by record buying from domestic institutions (DIIs), and a record ₹2.23 lakh crore was raised through 375 IPOs.

For Samvat 2083, the numbers to watch are FPI flows, crude oil and US-Iran tensions. Small caps already trade at much richer valuations than the Nifty 50.
