Sensex and Nifty post a third straight weekly gain, but Friday's fall shows the pressure 📊

The Sensex gained about 0.7% and the Nifty 50 about 0.5% in the week to Nov 13, 2026. On Friday the Sensex closed at 81,592.45 (-0.39%) and the Nifty at 25,144.30 (-0.61%), with every sector except FMCG ending lower.

Why it matters: retail inflation at 6.09% is above the RBI's 6% limit, crude oil is near $95 a barrel, the rupee is around 94.2 per dollar and US yields are higher. That weighs on rate-sensitive sectors like banks, realty and autos, even as foreign buying supports the market.

Next week, crude oil, the rupee and US Treasury yields are the key things to watch as Dalal Street reopens on Monday.
