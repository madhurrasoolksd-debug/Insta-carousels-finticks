India retail inflation hits 6.09% in October, above the RBI's 6% limit 📊

India's retail inflation (CPI) rose to 6.09% in October 2026 from 5.54% in September, a two-year high, with food inflation at 8.94% (7.74% in September), per MoSPI data released on Nov 12. In early trade on Nov 13 the Sensex was around 81,622 (-0.35%) and the Nifty 50 near 25,172 (-0.50%), with bank stocks leading the dip.

Why it matters: inflation above the RBI's 2-6% tolerance band raises the odds of tighter monetary policy, keeps loan rates higher for longer and squeezes household spending. Bank Nifty, autos and other rate-sensitive sectors are the ones to watch.
