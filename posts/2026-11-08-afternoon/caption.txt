Sensex and Nifty rally 10% while Wall Street crashes 📈

The Sensex has gained 10.62% in 7 straight sessions, closing at 81,028 on Friday. The Nifty 50 is back above 25,000 at 25,030. On Thursday, as the Nasdaq fell 5.52%, the Sensex rose 2.60%.

Why it matters: Indian stocks decoupled from a global sell-off. The drivers were heavy FII buying (₹14,975 crore on Thursday alone), strong Q2 bank and NBFC earnings, Brent crude easing to $96.82, and a firmer rupee.

The catch: the rally is narrow. Banks led, but more NSE stocks fell than rose on Friday, and small caps slipped. Samvat 2083 opens with today's Muhurat trading session. Crude oil and FII flows are the numbers to watch.
